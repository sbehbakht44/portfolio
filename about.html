Hi there!

Nice to meet you!

I’m Sara (she/her), a Seattle-based designer and researcher passionate about creating healthier, more human futures.

I’m a proud graduate of the University of Washington’s Human Centered Design & Engineering program, and I believe design can be a powerful force for positive social change. I keep people at the center of every decision I make.

As a Senior Interaction Designer at IDEO, I design products, services, and systems for clients across healthcare and education.

Outside of work, you can find me making pizza, running half-marathons, or making lop sided mugs :)
